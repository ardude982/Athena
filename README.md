# 📚 Athena

> **A smarter way to learn, plan, and make the most out of your time.**

Athena is an educational productivity platform designed to help students
organize their studies, manage their time, and make better use of their
daily effort.

The idea behind Athena is simple:

> **Spend less time figuring out what to do, and more time actually learning.**

## ✨ About Athena

Students often have to balance studying, assignments, exams, personal
projects, and everyday responsibilities.

Athena aims to bring useful educational and productivity tools together
in one organized environment.

With Athena, students can:

- 📅 Plan their study sessions
- ✅ Manage their tasks
- ⏱️ Track and manage their time
- 📝 Create and organize notes
- 🎯 Work toward their goals
- 🌱 Build better study habits
- 🧰 Access useful tools from one workspace

# 🎯 Why Athena?

Students often use multiple applications for different parts of their
daily workflow.

For example:

```text
Notes       → App A
Tasks       → App B
Timer       → App C
Planning    → App D


Athena aims to bring these tools together:
                 ┌─────────────────┐
                 │     ATHENA      │
                 ├─────────────────┤
                 │      Notes      │
                 │      Tasks      │
                 │     Planning    │
                 │      Timer      │
                 │      Tools      │
                 └─────────────────┘
The goal isn't simply to create another to-do list.

It's to create a more complete environment for students.
```
