# 📚 Athena

> **A smarter way to learn, plan, and make the most out of your time.**

Athena is an educational productivity platform designed to help students
organize their studies, manage their time, and make better use of their
daily effort.

The goal is simple:

**Spend less time figuring out what to do, and more time actually learning.**

---

## ✨ About the Project

Students often have to balance studying, assignments, exams, personal
projects, and everyday responsibilities.

Athena aims to bring these things together into one organized environment.

The platform combines educational and productivity-focused tools to help
students:

- 📅 Plan their study sessions
- ✅ Keep track of tasks
- ⏱️ Manage their time
- 📊 Organize their workload
- 📝 Keep notes and important information
- 🎯 Stay focused on their goals
- 🌱 Build better study habits

The project is designed around one main idea:

> **Your time and effort are valuable. Athena helps you use them wisely.**

# 🧰 Features

### 📋 Task Management

Create and organize tasks so important work doesn't get forgotten.

### 📝 Notes

Keep study material, ideas, and important information organized in one place.

### ⏱️ Time Management

Use timers and time-based tools to make study sessions more structured.

### 📅 Planning

Plan your workload and create a clearer path toward your academic goals.

### 🌦️ Useful Tools

Athena also brings practical tools into the workspace, allowing students
to access useful information without leaving the platform.
